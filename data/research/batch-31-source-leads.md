## Evidence routes

- Falsifiability and demarcation: Popper's primary texts, philosophy-of-science reference works, Bayesian and confirmation scholarship, and historical cases where auxiliary assumptions complicate simple tests.
- Method pluralism: Feyerabend's historical examples, contemporary philosophy of scientific practice, and evidence separating descriptive diversity from the normative claim that no methodological rules help.
- Intelligence measurement: primary psychometrics, reanalyses of Gould's Morton case, heritability and population-genetics guidance, and scholarship on race as a social and biological category.
- Skepticism and scientific literacy: misinformation, motivated reasoning, inoculation, critical-thinking transfer, and limits of generic baloney-detection heuristics.
- Deutsch's explanatory program: quantum foundations, computation, epistemology, universality, constructor theory, and the distinction between ambitious metaphysics and tested physics.
- Consilience: successful cross-level integration in biology and cognitive science alongside reductionism, emergence, historical contingency, and humanities-specific explanation.
- Scientific Revolution historiography: primary histories and specialist work on periodization, experiment, mathematics, instruments, institutions, colonial knowledge, and whether modern science was “invented.”

## Guardrails

- Do not score influence, rhetorical force, or philosophical elegance as empirical confirmation.
- Separate a proposed norm of inquiry from a descriptive history of how scientists actually worked.
- Treat demarcation as a family of problems; falsifiability is neither a complete scientific method nor useless.
- Preserve the difference between measurement bias, construct validity, score prediction, causal explanation, and group essentialism.
- Do not convert speculative unification or interpretation of quantum theory into settled experimental fact.
- Avoid both a single heroic birth of science and an account so diffuse that institutional and technical discontinuities disappear.
- Score exactly three central claims per book using the public four-state credit map and at least two independently useful sources per claim.
