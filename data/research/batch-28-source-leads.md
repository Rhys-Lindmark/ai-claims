# Rapid book batch 28 — cities and urbanism

Review state: rough AI review, source-traceable, not expert-reviewed.

## Scope

Ten influential books on cities, urban form, infrastructure, housing, public space, and segregation. Each record tests exactly three central claims under `three-central-claims/0.1.0`; scores are mechanically derived from claim credits rather than assigned independently.

## Evidence map

- **Urban form and activity:** peer-reviewed reviews and studies on Jacobs conditions, active streets, robbery, walkability, spatial legibility, and cognitive mapping.
- **Agglomeration and housing:** NBER syntheses, HUD regulatory-barrier material, World Bank urban-development material, and EPA smart-growth evidence.
- **Parking and infrastructure:** FHWA cruising and pricing reports plus EPA fiscal and infrastructure comparisons.
- **Social infrastructure:** CDC resources and peer-reviewed disaster, built-environment, and social-environment reviews.
- **Segregation:** HUD histories and training material, HUD lending archives, and the University of Richmond's digitized HOLC/FHA evidence collection.
- **Historical synthesis:** academic reviews of Mumford and Hall are used to distinguish documented examples from broad civilizational interpretation.

## Main uncertainty boundaries

- Built-environment associations are vulnerable to residential self-selection and correlated policy conditions.
- Historical syntheses select vivid cases and rarely supply representative counterfactuals.
- Fiscal effects depend on local accounting, tax authority, service standards, legacy assets, and intergovernmental transfers.
- Social infrastructure is difficult to randomize; access, programming, safety, and displacement mediate benefits.
- HOLC maps are strong evidence of discriminatory classification, but FHA maps and underwriting rules were often more directly tied to mortgage insurance decisions.

## Publication notes

- Ratings are deliberately coarse: supported, mostly supported, mixed, or weak.
- A high score does not mean every detail is correct or every policy prescription transfers.
- Cover enrichment must exact-match title and author; unresolved books retain the neutral placeholder.
