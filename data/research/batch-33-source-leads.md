## Evidence routes

- Race and punishment: Bureau of Justice Statistics, sentencing and drug-enforcement research, collateral-consequence law, voting restrictions, labor markets, and critiques of a single caste-system analogy.
- Wrongful conviction and capital punishment: exoneration registries, forensic-science reports, counsel quality, prosecutorial disclosure, innocence litigation, and case records behind Stevenson's narratives.
- Institutional design: charging and plea bargaining, local prosecutors, policing, defense funding, legislatures, federalism, court doctrine, and historical changes in crime and incarceration.
- Supreme Court accounts: opinions, voting data, archival histories, biographies, and explicit separation of reported private deliberation from public doctrine and causal impact.
- Civil-rights litigation: *Brown*, NAACP strategy, school segregation data, implementation, resistance, and limits of attributing social change to one judgment or legal team.
- Rule-of-law and legal positivism: jurisprudence scholarship, comparative indices, emergency powers, judicial independence, primary/secondary rules, discretion, and the boundary between conceptual and empirical claims.
- Incarceration drivers: admissions, sentence length, violent versus drug offenses, prosecutor discretion, state heterogeneity, crime trends, and decomposition of stocks versus flows.
- Misdemeanors: case volume, pretrial detention, fines and fees, counsel, plea pressure, warrants, collateral consequences, and local variation in lower courts.

## Guardrails

- Do not score a moral or constitutional argument as though it were an empirical claim.
- Separate racial disparity, discriminatory intent, institutional mechanism, and a totalizing caste analogy.
- Preserve differences among arrests, admissions, prison stocks, jail populations, sentence length, supervision, and collateral consequences.
- Treat individual cases as evidence of possibility and mechanism, not population prevalence.
- Do not infer Supreme Court causality from temporal sequence or private-conference reporting alone.
- Distinguish legal validity, moral legitimacy, institutional compliance, and practical access to remedies.
- Score exactly three central claims per book using the public four-state credit map and at least two independently useful sources per claim.
