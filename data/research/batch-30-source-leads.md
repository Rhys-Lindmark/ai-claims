## Evidence routes

- Democratic education and civic competence: philosophy-of-education scholarship and the 2026 review of democratic-competence studies.
- Critical pedagogy: direct analysis of Freire's method, literacy history, critical-pedagogy reviews, and evidence separating political aims from learning outcomes.
- Spiral curriculum and discovery: Bruner scholarship, guided-versus-unguided instruction reviews, cognitive-load research, and curriculum studies.
- Constructionism and computation: computational-thinking reviews, Logo/Mindstorms histories, and evaluations that distinguish engagement from transferable learning.
- Student misconceptions and cognition: cognitive-science reviews of memory, prior knowledge, motivation, retrieval, spacing, interleaving, and feedback.
- Curriculum and international comparison: reading and knowledge-building studies, OECD/PISA documentation, and criticism of cross-country narrative inference.
- Meta-analysis: Hattie's underlying syntheses, critiques of incomparable effect sizes and the hinge point, plus domain-specific replication.

## Guardrails

- Do not equate influence, classroom enthusiasm, or philosophical appeal with measured learning.
- Separate minimally guided discovery from guided inquiry and explicit instruction from passive lecturing.
- Treat international test comparisons as observational and institutionally confounded.
- Preserve outcome, age, subject, duration, implementation, and equity differences hidden by aggregate effect sizes.
- Score only three central claims per book and keep broader political or moral aims outside the factual denominator unless they make testable predictions.
